Privacy

What stays on this Mac. Your orgs’ details, logs, tickets, code, chats and memory are kept in Telltale’s folder on this Mac. Sign-ins are kept in your Keychain. Telltale has no account of its own.

What goes to your AI model. When you ask, your question and the context it needs go to the AI model you connected in Settings › Models. Telltale masks the personal details and secrets it recognises; it can miss a value, and pictures are sent as they are. A project set to Unmasked sends its log text unmasked.

What goes to the services you connect. Telltale reads Salesforce, and Jira, Slack, Confluence, GitHub and the other services you connect, with your own sign-ins. Something leaves for one of them, such as a chat reply, only when you click Send, or on its own where you turned that on, after a 5-second Cancel.

What reaches Telltale’s makers. Nothing from your orgs, logs, tickets, code or chats, and no usage statistics or crash reports. Telltale checks GitHub for a new version; that request carries nothing about you or your work.

The download page. It counts downloads by country, with no cookie. To count a click once, a one-way hash of your address is kept for an hour, then dropped. The feedback form uses a Cloudflare check and keeps your IP address for up to an hour to stop abuse, and what you write is posted as a public GitHub issue, so leave out anything private.
